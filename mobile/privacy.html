CongoLingo mobile app privacy policy

Webynn LLC operates CongoLingo. This notice covers the mobile learning app and requests sent through its support form. Updated November 24, 2026.

Lessons and accounts

As a guest, your device stores your learning progress and preferences. The app contacts our service to load published lessons and recordings. If you create a learner account, we store your name, email, account identifier and learning progress so you can sign in and continue on another device. For password accounts, we store a password hash. If you use Google sign-in, Google provides an identifier, verified email and name to authenticate your account.

Who processes information

Our service runs on Railway. Authorized Studio staff can view learner profiles and progress to support learners and manage the service. Network providers receive technical information such as an IP address when your device requests content. When a satellite map is loaded from Mapbox, your device requests an image of the lesson map from Mapbox.

Support and testing

If you send feedback, the team receives the contact information, device details, message and optional images you submit. We use these to investigate issues and respond. If you volunteer to test the app, we use your signup details to arrange testing and may share your store email with Apple or Google to deliver your invitation.

Control over your information

You may use guest mode without providing an account email. Families can use this option on a shared device. You can delete a learner account from Profile. For help accessing or deleting account data, or to request removal of a separate feedback submission or tester signup, contact Webynn LLC through our support form. A parent or guardian can use the same form to ask about a child’s information. Please omit passwords and unnecessary personal details.
